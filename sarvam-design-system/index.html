Foundations · 02

Colour

A neutral, light-first canvas. Brand colour arrives as light: indigo meets saffron. About 85% neutrals, 10% spectrum, 4% slate, 1% accents.

Foundations · 14

Soft-glow surfaces

The pastel haze at the bottom of Sarvam's cards and posts: a solid single-hue shape with a curved top edge, put through a Gaussian blur, so the fade follows the curve. It is not a linear or radial gradient. Made with tools/soft-glow/ (CLI + visual generator).

Card set: one hue per card, different contour per neighbour

Foundations · 15

Glyph colour depth

New colourways for Sarvam's gateway shapes using colour alone: a gradient core plus a soft rim that follows every lobe and cusp. Four rim moods: white (outer depth), colour (inner depth), shadow (form curves away) and luminous (the edge glows). Plus echo, dusk inside a gateway. Shapes are the official glyphs and the gateway arch; palettes are sr-* tokens. Made with tools/color-depth/.

Foundations · 08 · style 1

Icons · flat stroke

Hugeicons (confirmed in sarvam.ai's bundles), 1.5px stroke in currentColor. For dense UI: nav, rows, buttons, tabs. Iconify supplies third-party logos.

Foundations · 08 · style 2

Icons · solid product hue

One flat fill in the product's hue, with inner details knocked out. This is how sarvam.ai colour-codes products: TTS periwinkle, STT coral, Translation orange, Parse pink, verified indigo. Use for product tiles, feature lists and badges.

Foundations · 08 · style 3

Icons · depth (periwinkle)

Matches sarvam.ai: always one periwinkle, never tinted per product. One 172° gradient across the icon box (#a8b6ef → #859ae7 → #627ddf), a white top sheen, a subtle inner rim, and white knock-outs. For illustrations, use-case cards and marketing at 40px and above. To colour-code, use the solid style.
