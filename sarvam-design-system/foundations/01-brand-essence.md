# 01 · Brand Essence

## Mission and positioning
- **Tagline:** *AI for all from India* (no comma on the website; the brand page uses "AI for all, from India" as a display line. Both are fine, but stay consistent within one piece.)
- **What Sarvam is:** India's full-stack **sovereign AI platform**: foundation models, voice and language APIs, and agent products, built and operated in India.
- **Official descriptor (press):** "an Indic language AI company". Mission: "make AI accessible and useful for every Indian language speaker."
- **Name:** *Sarvam* = "all" in Sanskrit, meaning wholeness: everything, everyone, everywhere.

## The big idea: The Gateway Between Worlds
> "Architectural gateways are a familiar presence across Indian life… not as decoration, but as structure… A gateway represents a moment of transition, where two worlds move toward each other. Human and machine. Culture and computation. What already exists and what is still emerging." (sarvam.ai/brand)

How this shows up in design:
| Concept | Visual expression |
|---|---|
| Gateway / threshold | Cusped multifoil arch silhouettes; headlines framed inside an arch; content "emerging" through an opening |
| Two worlds meeting | **Blue→orange spectrum**: cool indigo (machine, depth) blending into warm saffron (human, culture) |
| Mandala construction | Monogram from repeated circles; glyphs built from concentric, layered offsets; radial symmetry |
| Movement and transformation | Continuous gradients, never discrete colour blocks; soft glows that feel alive and responsive |

## Brand pillars (from sarvam.ai "Why Sarvam")
1. **Sovereign by design.** Build, deploy and run AI with full control, developed and operated entirely in India.
2. **State-of-the-art models.** Built for India's languages, culture and context.
3. **Human at the core.** Forward-deployed engineers work alongside your teams.

Supporting themes: *population-scale impact*, *enterprise-grade out of the box*, *built to run anywhere your business runs* (Cloud / VPC / On-prem).

## Design principles
1. **Structure over ornament.** Culture is expressed through form (arches, mandala geometry, rhythm), not decoration for its own sake.
2. **Light over blocks.** Colour behaves like light: gradients, glows, transitions.
3. **Breadth with cohesion.** One system for many products. Product identity comes from glyph colour and imagery, not new styles.
4. **Patience and layering.** Like a mandala, compositions reward a second look: concentric layers, inner shadows, subtle grain.
5. **Clarity first.** Lots of whitespace, centred section titles, short copy, one primary action.
