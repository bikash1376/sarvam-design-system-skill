# 13 · Voice and Tone

> Length budgets, the four-element hero, banned words and believable demo data live in **`16-copywriting.md`**. This file is the voice; that one is the craft.

## Voice in one line
**Confident, plain-spoken and quietly proud: frontier technology described in calm, human sentences, rooted in India.**

| Attribute | Means | Example (real Sarvam copy) |
|---|---|---|
| **Declarative** | Short statements, no hedging | "AI for all from India." · "India Can." |
| **Concrete** | Specific outcomes, real numbers | "Natural voices across 11 Indic languages" · "<500ms real-time latency" |
| **Calm confidence** | No hype words, no exclamation marks | "Enterprise-grade. Out of the box." |
| **Human** | People and partnership | "Human at the core." · "Not a handoff. A partnership." |
| **Rooted** | India as builder, not backdrop | "The AI Platform India Builds On" · "Powering India's AI-first future" |

## Headline formulas
1. **Statement of belief:** `AI for all from India` · `India Can`
2. **Platform claim:** `The AI Platform India Builds On` · `India's Full-Stack Sovereign AI Platform`
3. **Outcome + qualifier:** `Voice agents that sound human and deliver results`
4. **Verb-led invitation:** `Build anything with Sarvam APIs` · `Build the Future of India's AI with Sarvam`
5. **Two-beat fragment:** `Enterprise-grade. Out of the box.` · `Build. Test. Go live.`
6. **Personal benefit:** `Intelligence, built around you.`
7. **Event/drop teaser:** `India's AI / Now Arriving` · `India's Frontier AI Conference`

Headlines: **2–7 words**, sentence case, a full stop is allowed for two-beat fragments, never "!".

## Body copy
- 1–2 sentences per card (≤ 25 words). Lead with the benefit, then the proof.
- Card descriptions may start lowercase when they continue the title ("Sovereign by design — build, deploy, and run AI with full control…"). This is a site quirk; use it only in pillar lists.
- Prefer "you/your teams". "We" is fine in announcements.
- Spell in **Indian/British English**: "optimisation", "organisation", "programme" (social posts mix in "program" for named programmes like "Champions Program"; keep official names as published).

2–3 words, Title-ish capitalisation as on the site ("Get Started", "Contact Us").

## Social copy pattern (LinkedIn/X)
```
[1-line hook stating what launched or happened.]

[2–3 sentences: what it does, for whom, why it matters for India.]

[Optional bullets of capabilities, no emoji bullets.]

[CTA line + link]
```
Examples: "Today, we're launching the Sarvam Startup Program." · "Introducing Sarvam Champions." · "Join us for the next drop. This time, in-person."

## Naming rules (official)
- "Sarvam AI" on first mention, then "Sarvam".
- Exact model names (as on sarvam.ai/models): Bulbul V4 / Bulbul V4 Flash (TTS), Saaras V4 (STT), Sarvam Translate, Sarvam Vision, Sarvam 105B. Version suffixes are written "V4", with a space.
- Exact product names: Indus, Indus Cowork, Sarvam Voice Agents, Document AI, Doc Agents, Work Agents, Coding Agents, Content Studio, Sarvam Edge, Token Factory, Sarvam Champions, Sarvam Startup Program, Sarvam Epoch.
- Partners say "powered by Sarvam AI" / "built with Sarvam AI's speech-to-text API".

## Words to use / avoid
| Use | Avoid |
|---|---|
| sovereign, population-scale, Indic, frontier, built for India, every Indian language, production-ready, forward-deployed | revolutionary, unleash, supercharge, magic, cutting-edge, disrupt, next-gen, world-class (overused), "Bharat" as a gimmick |
