# 09 · Imagery

Sarvam's images show **real India, with modern framing**. Four families, from most to least common:

## 1. Documentary photography of Indian life
- Real people at work and in daily life: farmers, artisans, dancers, commuters, shopkeepers, families.
- **Natural light, rich but not oversaturated colour, shallow depth of field, slight motion blur** (the dancers on the beach).
- Often **cropped by a gateway shape**: the photo sits inside an arch-shaped mask, or a deep-colour gateway panel (forest `#293A18`, sindoor-maroon `#6b1a14`) holds a Season Mix line like "In your own words" next to the photo.
- Regional diversity matters. Avoid a single-region cliché (it's "for all").

## 2. Material and texture close-ups (heritage palette)
Painted temple doors, bandhani fabric, marigolds, block-print textiles, sandstone carving. These are shown as **vertical strips** with a hex swatch chip (`● #293A18`), which proves that the palette comes from real Indian surfaces.
Heritage swatches: `#293A18` forest, `#FEBF2B` marigold, `#9E5F0A` ochre, `#C11317` sindoor.

## 3. Monochrome 3D renders
Clean single-object renders (a chess piece, a device, a microphone) in **one brand hue** (indigo `#4250d5` to `#2b2fb8`). Secondary objects are ghosted in white and fade out. The background is pure white. Used for program and community announcements ("Champions Program").

## 4. Halftone and pixel-textile (events only)
For flagship events (Sarvam Epoch): **black-and-white halftone** photos of Indian architecture (chhatris, domes) combined with **pixelated textile bands**: magenta/marigold check patterns, saffron squares with concentric pixel circles. Bold and festive. Use it only for event key visuals.

## Product UI as imagery
Product shots show real UI (composer box, research doc, voice-agent call) on **white cards with a 16px radius**, floating over a periwinkle aura with a soft shadow. Use UI frames with tabs (Indus Cowork · Voice Agents · Text to Speech · Speech to Text · Parse).

## Rules
- ✅ Real Indian people, places and materials; diverse regions, ages and contexts
- ✅ Gateway-shaped crops; captions in Season Mix
- ✅ Indic script in the image (signage, handwritten notes) where natural
- ❌ Generic stock "AI" imagery: glowing brains, robots, blue circuit boards, holographic faces
- ❌ AI-generated people (uncanny hands and faces), especially for testimonials
- ❌ Poverty tourism, exoticised festival shots, Taj Mahal skylines
- ❌ Heavy filters, duotones in non-brand colours, vignettes

## Image-generation prompt suffix (when no photo is available)
> "…documentary photograph, natural window light, shallow depth of field, warm neutral grade, India, authentic everyday setting, no text, no logos, no AI-glow effects, 35mm, editorial, calm composition with negative space on the left."

For 3D: "…single [object] rendered in matte indigo (#4250D5) monochrome, soft studio light, pure white background, ghosted white duplicates behind fading out, minimal, no text."
